Privacy & trust

How your data is handled

Plain language, no marketing. This page describes what the software in this version actually does.

What we store

  • The Excel file you upload, exactly as sent, so your download can keep your original styles and sheet order.
  • A working copy of your sheets, columns and records (JSON), plus your field settings and saved charts.
  • A short list of recent snapshots used for Undo.
  • An anonymous random session cookie so only your browser can open your workbooks.

Files are kept in a temporary folder on the server. There is no database and no account.

How it is processed

  • Everything, including field detection, charts and the plain-language explanations, is calculated by ordinary code on this server.
  • Your data is not sent to any AI service or third party. The app works fully without any AI key.
  • Spreadsheet content is treated as untrusted. Macros are rejected and formulas are never executed by the server.

How long we keep it

Each workbook expires automatically after a period of inactivity (24 hours by default on this server) and is then deleted together with its files. A background job removes expired workspaces regularly.

Deleting your data

Open your workbook and choose Delete workbook from the menu. This removes the uploaded file, the working copy and the undo history immediately.

Security measures in place

  • Workspace IDs are random 128-bit values and are checked against your session on every request. Someone else's ID returns "not found".
  • Upload checks on file size, extension, file signature and zip structure; macros and decompression bombs are refused.
  • Server-side validation of every value you save, and protection against spreadsheet formula injection in CSV downloads.
  • Security headers, a strict content-security policy, rate limiting, same-site cookies and request-origin checks.
  • Error messages never reveal server paths or internal details.
What we do not claim: this is not end-to-end encrypted and has not been independently audited. Do not upload data you are not allowed to share with the operator of this server. For public hosting, always serve it over HTTPS.

Known limitations

  • .xls (old format) is not supported. Re-save as .xlsx in Excel first.
  • Excel features that may not survive the download: pivot tables, charts and pictures inside the workbook, slicers, form controls, some conditional-formatting rules, and macros (rejected on upload). The data and your edits are always included.
  • Formulas are kept as formulas until you overwrite that cell, but results are only recalculated when you open the file in Excel or another spreadsheet app.
  • Merged cells or Excel "Tables" that overlap your data table are separated or removed in the download. We show a warning when this applies.
  • Header detection is a best guess. If your column names are not in the first filled row, pick the right header row in the workbook overview.
  • Inferred field types are suggestions. Please review them before relying on them.
  • Charts describe patterns in your data. They do not prove that one thing causes another.
  • This version is built for one person per workbook. Real-time team collaboration, accounts and shared workspaces are not available yet.

If you spot something this page gets wrong, please tell us. Accuracy matters more than sounding impressive.
