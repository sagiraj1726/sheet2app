Automatic field detection

It reads your data, not just your headers

Sheet2App looks at column names, the values inside, how often they repeat and whether they are dates, numbers or yes/no answers, then picks the clearest control.

Guesses are only suggestions. Every field shows why it was chosen, and you can override any of them. When in doubt we use a safe text field rather than quietly changing your data.

Your IDs are safe. Survey numbers, phone numbers, postal codes and account numbers are never turned into ordinary numbers.

Questions

Frequently asked

What kind of Excel files work?

Modern .xlsx workbooks with one or many sheets. Old .xls files are not supported yet: open them in Excel and use File → Save As → Excel Workbook. Macro-enabled files are rejected for safety.

Will my formulas and formatting survive?

Formulas, column widths, styles and sheet order are kept where possible, and formula results refresh when you open the file in Excel. Some advanced items (charts, pictures and pivot tables inside the workbook) may not survive the download. The privacy page lists known limits openly.

Do I need an account?

No. You get an anonymous private workspace in your browser. Team accounts and sharing are planned but not available yet.

Are the charts "AI generated"?

No. Charts and explanations are produced by deterministic calculations on your data, so the numbers always match your sheet. The app works fully without any AI service.

Is it really free?

The Free plan is open now. Paid plans are not yet on sale; the prices shown are hypotheses we are testing, and you will be told clearly before anything is ever charged.
