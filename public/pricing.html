    Pricing questions

    Why are the paid prices shown as ranges?

    They are hypotheses. We want to learn what is fair for small businesses, teachers and teams before fixing a price. Nothing is billed now.

    What happens when I reach a limit?

    You will see a clear message saying which limit you reached and what to do, for example deleting an old workbook. Your existing data is never held hostage or deleted because of a limit.

    Will there be a payment option?

    Only after real checkout, verification and plan-checking are in place. Until then the "Notify me" buttons simply record interest on this device. No card details are ever requested.

    Can I keep my data if I stop using it?

    Yes. You can download your updated Excel at any time. Your file is yours; we only keep a temporary working copy.
